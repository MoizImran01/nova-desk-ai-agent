# Transfer conversation control when a handoff is requested

A patient who asks for a person starts a human handoff immediately. When the master FAQ cannot answer a question, Nova offers a handoff and waits for the patient to accept. Nova saves the request to the clinic’s shared front-desk inbox before acknowledging it. After-hours requests stay in the inbox.

Once the handoff is saved, Nova sends the acknowledgement and pauses automated replies and appointment changes. Staff receive subsequent patient messages in the same conversation and can reply there. Nova resumes only when a staff member explicitly hands the conversation back. This keeps a follow-up such as “move me to Friday” visible to staff while the patient’s existing appointment stays booked.
