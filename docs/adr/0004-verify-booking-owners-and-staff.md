# Verify booking owners by email and staff by individual account

Patients can ask general FAQs and see available times without signing in. Before a patient confirms, views, moves or cancels an appointment, Nova verifies control of the booking email using a one-time code sent to that address. Verification applies to that patient and email for the conversation, so a patient can finish a multi-turn booking without entering a new code at each step.

Each staff member signs in with an individual account and a second factor. Front-desk staff can claim and answer handoffs. Designated clinic admins can also replace the master FAQ. Attribute each staff action to the individual who performed it.
